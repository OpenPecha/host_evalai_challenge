Welcome to the STT-Challenge

Challenge Overview:

Tashi Change Welcome to the Transcription Accuracy Challenge! This challenge is designed to evaluate the accuracy of automatic transcription systems. Participants will work on a dataset of audio transcriptions and submit their outputs in JSON format. The goal is to achieve the highest transcription accuracy based on two critical metrics: Word Error Rate (WER) and Character Error Rate (CER).

Challenge Format:

  • Participants will be provided with a ground truth dataset.
  • The evaluation script will compute the WER and CER of the submissions compared to the ground truth.
  • Submissions will be ranked based on their accuracy scores.

Objective:

The challenge aims to encourage innovation in speech recognition and transcription models by providing a robust evaluation framework. Whether you are fine-tuning pre-trained models or building systems from scratch, this is your chance to showcase your expertise!

Good luck, and may the best transcription win!
