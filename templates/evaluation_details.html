Evaluation Metrics:

The evaluation script calculates two key metrics: Word Error Rate (WER) and Character Error Rate (CER). These metrics compare the ground truth transcriptions with the user-submitted transcriptions to assess accuracy.

Process:

  • Validates the submission format to ensure all required keys are present and values are strings.
  • Calculates the average WER and CER across all samples in the dataset.

Outcome:

The results provide a comprehensive view of transcription accuracy, enabling fair and effective evaluation of submissions.
